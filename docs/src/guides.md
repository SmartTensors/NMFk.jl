# Guides and research

The focused manuals and research notes below remain maintained as reviewable Markdown beside their supporting scripts and figures in the NMFk repository.

- [Grid-data manual](https://github.com/SmartTensors/NMFk.jl/blob/master/docs/griddata_manual.md) explains regular-grid construction from scattered coordinates and values.
- [Mapbox manual](https://github.com/SmartTensors/NMFk.jl/blob/master/docs/mapbox_manual.md) covers interactive spatial plots and figure export.
- [Mapbox contour manual](https://github.com/SmartTensors/NMFk.jl/blob/master/docs/mapbox_contour_manual.md) covers inverse-distance interpolation and continuous contour maps.
- [Tensor information metrics](https://github.com/SmartTensors/NMFk.jl/blob/master/docs/tensor_information_metrics.md) defines the structure-aware tensor, lag, residual-coding, and raw-to-grid measures.
- [Information-preserving tensor discretization](https://github.com/SmartTensors/NMFk.jl/blob/master/docs/tensor_information_research_paper.md) presents the research framework and Oklahoma case study.

These documents contain domain-scale examples and figure-generation workflows, so the documentation build links to them without executing expensive analyses or regenerating scientific artifacts.
